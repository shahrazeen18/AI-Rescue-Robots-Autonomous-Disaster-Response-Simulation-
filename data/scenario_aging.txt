# reported at t=0) competes with a stream of urgency-5 requests (one every 2
# steps, each needing 2 steps of work), so the robot is never idle. Without
# aging (PARAM aging_steps 0) request 1 waits until the stream ends (t~62);
# with aging_steps 10 its effective urgency reaches 5 at t=40 and, being the
